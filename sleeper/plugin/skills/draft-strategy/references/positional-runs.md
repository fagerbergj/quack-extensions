# Positional runs - follow vs. break

Source: `research-r9-drafting.md` §1, "Positional runs - follow vs break".

## Definition

A positional run = players from the same position picked consecutively
(The Fantasy Footballers, [Aug 19, 2021](https://www.thefantasyfootballers.com/articles/run-the-runs-timing-picks-in-fantasy-football-drafts/) -
2021 data, treat coefficients as illustrative, not current).

## Signals

Run-continuation: each WR pick in the previous round predicts +0.5
expected WR picks this round (significant); the RB equivalent is +0.18,
not significant; QB picks predict -0.42 QB picks next round (thin QB
tiers - managers punt once the studs go) (same source). Run-exhaustion:
every position shows an inverse relationship between how much has been
taken across the whole draft and expected picks this round, largest at
TE and QB (same source). The 10-team a priori run expectation: ~12-13
RBs among the first 22 picks ([PFF, Sep 7, 2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-perfect-draft-strategy-round-by-round-10-team-leagues)) -
check whether the room is ahead of or behind that line.

## 2026 resolution: break at the tier cliff, not run length

"If you see a run but your tier still has depth, you hold your
ground... Managers without tiers panic. Managers with tiers stay calm"
([Fantasy Football Almanac, Feb 19, 2026](https://thefantasyfootballalmanac.com/2026/02/19/fantasy-football-draft-tiers-explained-2026-strategy-guide/)).
PFF builds its whole 10-team plan on this: RBs through the top-15 tier,
then load WR rounds 3-6 ([PFF, Sep 7, 2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-perfect-draft-strategy-round-by-round-10-team-leagues)).
No source publishes a hard "break after N consecutive picks" threshold;
the defensible procedure is marginal tier drop-off, not run length.

## The overpayment tax

Picking at the end of a run means taking that player 0.25 ADP spots
earlier than he usually goes - the tax on hesitation ([Fantasy Footballers, Aug 19, 2021](https://www.thefantasyfootballers.com/articles/run-the-runs-timing-picks-in-fantasy-football-drafts/)).

## The mirror-image error

"The important thing here is not reaching because you're afraid of what
will happen before [your next pick]" - the counter-risk to breaking a
run out of fear is reaching ([FanDuel Research, 2026, no visible date](https://www.fanduel.com/research/fantasy-football-advice-draft-strategy-for-every-draft-slot-in-a-12-team-ppr-league)).

## Don't chase

"Don't draft a quarterback because everyone else did... The managers
who win year after year don't chase runs. They let everyone else panic,
then capitalize on the value those managers leave behind" (Jeff
Ratcliffe, FTN, [Jul 29, 2026](https://ftnfantasy.com/nfl/5-draft-day-mistakes-even-good-fantasy-managers-still-make)).
The one documented exception: a true tier cliff - "pounce early on the
last player left in a positional tier" (Clay, ESPN, [Jun 3, 2026](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/48937876/2026-fantasy-football-draft-secrets-tips-nfl)).
