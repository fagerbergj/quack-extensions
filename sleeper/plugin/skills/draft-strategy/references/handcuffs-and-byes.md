# Handcuffs and bye weeks

Source: `research-r9-drafting.md` §1, "Handcuffs and bye weeks".

## Handcuffs: base rate is weak

Only 23% of handcuff RBs drafted in rounds 10-15 were "hits" (defined as
≥2 startable 10-pt weeks, mainly while the starter was out); 59% misses,
18% partial (Fantasy Dutchman, [2025](https://thefantasydutchman.com/drafting-handcuff-rbs-valuable-or-just-a-myth/)).
Opportunity cost: rounds-10-15 WRs outscored rounds-10-15 RBs by ~18
points/season, 2019-24 (same source).

## The two-condition checklist

Draft a handcuff only if (1) the RB1 has a multi-week injury history AND
(2) the backup has proven production when the starter is out; otherwise
value the late-round WR - the waiver wire (FAAB, in this format) often
delivers the same insurance cheaper (Fantasy Dutchman, [2025](https://thefantasydutchman.com/drafting-handcuff-rbs-valuable-or-just-a-myth/)).

## Never your own, by default

Handcuffing your own RB1 is "drafting for the worst-case scenario; it's
drafting scared"; if drafting a backup, draft it behind *other*
managers' RB1s. Handcuffing WR/TE/QB in redraft is "almost always an
inoptimal use of resources" (Ted Chmyz, FantasySP, [Jul 28, 2025](https://www.fantasysp.com/news/nfl/3055123/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-what-is)).

## Disagreement

Three different answers exist: never your own (Chmyz, above);
conditional yes on the two-condition checklist (Fantasy Dutchman,
above); or "play-not-to-lose" bounded floor insurance behind an
injury-prone starter ([Fantasy Football Blueprint, Aug 6, 2026, summary-only](https://www.fantasyfootballblueprint.com/2026/08/06/10-handcuffing-running-backs/)).
The disagreement is about expected value, not facts - the same 23%
hit-rate data supports "rarely worth it" and "cheap insurance when it
fires" both.

## Bye weeks: the onesie rule

Teams with all QBs on the same bye advanced at 14.1% vs. the 16.7%
benchmark - a 2.6% advance-rate penalty (same for TE rooms); never draft
a QB with the same bye as QB1, always draft TEs with unique byes
(Stephen Hoopes, 4for4, [May 30, 2025](https://www.4for4.com/2025/preseason/best-ball-using-schedule-your-advantage)).

## Which week matters as much as stacking

ESPN's 2026 Bye Week Scarcity Meter: Week 5 = 2 (6% of elite talent
off), Week 11 = 6 (six-team bye), **Week 13 = the season's
"Bye-mageddon," 16% of elite talent off** (Tristan H. Cockcroft, ESPN,
[May 15, 2026, updated May 22, 2026](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/48778888/fantasy-football-bye-week-scarcity-meter-weighing-impact-the-nine-bye-weeks-2026)).

## Disagreement: stack vs. spread

Deliberate stack: intentionally target same-bye players, surrender one
gutted week ([SI.com, Aug 14, 2025](https://www.si.com/onsi/fantasy/nfl/bye-week-blues-navigating-a-bye-week-heavy-strategy)).
Spread/never-stack-onesies: the 2.6% penalty above. No source quantifies
a stacked bye's expected point loss in a 10-team redraft league
specifically - a stated gap, not papered over. Unique byes are mandatory
at QB and TE; at RB/WR a deliberate single-bye stack is defensible only
in a cheap (low Scarcity-Meter) week, never Week 13.
