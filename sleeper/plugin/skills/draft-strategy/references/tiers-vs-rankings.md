# Tiers vs. rankings

Source: `research-r9-drafting.md` §1, "Tiers - construction and use".

## What a tier is

A tier is a cluster of similar projected value; a tier break occurs
where the projected point drop between consecutive players is
significant - a worked example is a 29-point projected drop from RB3 to
RB4 as a natural cutoff ([Fantasy Football Almanac, Feb 19, 2026](https://thefantasyfootballalmanac.com/2026/02/19/fantasy-football-draft-tiers-explained-2026-strategy-guide/)).
Tier width must be uneven - if every tier has roughly the same player
count, "that is a massive red flag" (same source). Quantified cliff,
2026 cycle: McBride TE VOR 89.0, Bowers 82.6, then Loveland 42.2 - a
40-point TE2-to-TE3 drop ([YAFSB, Mar 11, 2026](https://yafsb.com/content/vor-value-over-replacement-fantasy-football/)).

## Why tiers beat rankings

"Trying to precisely rank every single player... is an impossible task.
Football is a volatile sport... When you treat rankings as gospel, you
fail to acknowledge this inherent chaos" (Pete Overzet, Fantasy Life,
[Jul 10, 2024, updated Jun 22, 2025](https://www.fantasylife.com/articles/best-ball/forget-ranks-tiers-and-humility-based-drafting-are-the-way)).
Cost-of-precision example: Jalen Reagor and Justin Jefferson had the
same 2020 ADP range; Jefferson went 88-1400-7, Reagor 31-396-1.

## Operational rules

- Within a tier, preference is a coin flip; reaching is only bad across
  tiers, sacrificing ADP value (same source).
- **One-left-in-the-tier rule:** if one player remains in the higher
  tier at one position and several remain in the lower tier at another,
  draft the higher tier ([Almanac, Feb 19, 2026](https://thefantasyfootballalmanac.com/2026/02/19/fantasy-football-draft-tiers-explained-2026-strategy-guide/)).
- If multiple available players share a tier, prioritize other
  positions; if a tier is about to dry up, act quickly ([ESPN, Jun 10, 2026](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/49000804/fantasy-football-make-six-draft-mistakes)).
- "A ranking tells you who is first. A tier tells you how close the
  choices actually are... Rankings tell you who. Tiers tell you when"
  (Almanac, same source).

## Disagreement on granularity

Wide tiers around upside (FantasyPros ECR style) vs. tighter groups
"that reflect hard lines in value" (Chris Welsh, per FantasyPros'
own tier pages, [Jul 2025, snippet-only](https://www.fantasypros.com/2025/07/fantasy-football-tiers-must-haves-busts-draft-values/)).
Reconcile by encoding tiers as bands with cliffs, not a rigid order.
