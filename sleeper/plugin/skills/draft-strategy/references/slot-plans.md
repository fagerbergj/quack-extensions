# Pick slot - early / middle / late

Source: `research-r9-drafting.md` §1, "Pick slot - early / middle / late across the first three turns".

## Seat value (10-team PPR, 2026)

By ADP: Pick 3 is the best seat (331.3 3D-Value points); Pick 9 the
worst (284.7); Pick 8 is 14.1% poorer than Pick 3 through six rounds.
Drafting optimally, the best seat flips to Pick 4 (340.3); "Picks 8, 9,
and 10 are the three worst" (Jared Smola, DraftSharks, [Aug 26, 2026](https://www.draftsharks.com/article/best-draft-position-fantasy-football-10-team)).
Optimal play nets +2.2% to +10.1% more 3D Value than straight ADP at
every seat - direct quantification of the value of live judgment over
ADP.

## Early (1-3): plan for the long wait

"The more important part of drafting first is planning for Picks 24 and
25. You won't select again for nearly two rounds" ([FanDuel Research, 2026, no visible date](https://www.fanduel.com/research/fantasy-football-advice-draft-strategy-for-every-draft-slot-in-a-12-team-ppr-league)) -
in 10-team terms that wait is picks 11-20; pre-commit the second-turn
plan to two possible first-pick scenarios. Anti-reach rule: don't reach
because you're afraid of what will happen before your next pick (same
source).

## Middle (4-7): flexibility and falling value

"Might be the most flexible draft position... you're never waiting more
than 12 selections between picks" (FanDuel Research, same source); "the
worst thing you can do at No. 8 is decide beforehand that you're taking
one specific position" (same source). "Middle picks offer flexibility
and the best access to falling value" ([Almanac, Feb 23, 2026](https://thefantasyfootballalmanac.com/2026/02/23/fantasy-football-draft-order-strategy/)).

## Late (8-10): turn control, plan in pairs

Think in pairs - "which two-player combination can I reasonably build
with Picks 10 and 15?"; don't force RB-WR balance if two receivers are
clearly the best available (FanDuel Research, same source). Late picks
"start at a deficit... forces a riskier draft strategy" - load
high-risk/high-reward picks to counter the early-slot edge
([FantasySharks, Jul 14, 2025](https://www.fantasysharks.com/draft-science-best-snake-draft-position-2025/)).
A second QB early is a unique edge for late first-round pickers ([PFF pick-10 companion, Aug 2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-2026-perfect-draft-strategy-round-by-round-10-team-leagues-pick-10)).

## Disagreement: no two sources rank all 10 seats identically

DraftSharks: Pick 4 by optimal play / Pick 3 by ADP; FantasySharks: "one
of the first four," with 7-8 better than the 12-team analogy suggests;
FanDuel calls No. 3 "one of my favorite places to draft." Report slot
value as directional and seat-specific, never a single hard-coded "best
slot."
