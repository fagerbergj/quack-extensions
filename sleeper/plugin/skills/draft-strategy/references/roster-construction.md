# Roster construction - positional timing (10-team PPR)

Source: `research-r9-drafting.md` §1, "Roster construction - positional timing rules".

## RB

10-team run expectation: "roughly 12 or 13 of the first 22 picks will be
running backs" (Nathan Jahnke, PFF, [Sep 7, 2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-perfect-draft-strategy-round-by-round-10-team-leagues)) -
use as an a priori run-length check: if by pick 22 fewer RBs have gone,
the run is lagging and RBs may still fall. Hero RB numbers: the 2024 top
4 RBs posted 336+ PPR points (Gibbs 362.9, Barkley 355.3, Bijan 341.7,
Henry 336.4) while RBs 5-21 all finished 200-300 ([FantasyLabs, Aug 18, 2025](https://www.fantasylabs.com/articles/hero-rb-explained-top-fantasy-football-targets-for-2025/)).
Zero RB is only viable *as long as* RBs going after round 4 significantly
outperform ADP ([Fantasy Life, Jul 4, 2025](https://www.fantasylife.com/articles/fantasy/zero-rb-strategy-for-fantasy-football-2025-alvin-kamara-and-more-league-winning-targets));
the 10-team-specific verdict is "don't do Zero RB - the numbers do not
add up" ([Sporting News, 2025, snippet-only](https://www.sportingnews.com/us/fantasy/news/fantasy-football-10-team-draft-strategy-2025/89641aec2e5552e3f3bad15c)).

## WR

A 10-team league has room for three "set-and-forget" WRs, rounds 3-6
([PFF, Sep 7, 2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-perfect-draft-strategy-round-by-round-10-team-leagues)).
"In 10-team you don't need to draft a WR in the first round, even if it
is a PPR league" - but load to ~3 before round 6 ([Sporting News, 2025, snippet-only](https://www.sportingnews.com/us/fantasy/news/fantasy-football-10-team-draft-strategy-2025/89641aec2e5552e3f3bad15c)).

## QB (1QB, 10-team)

The usable market compresses: with 10 teams the last starting-slot QB is
QB10; "waiting on QB" realistically means rounds 8-12 ([FFC 10-team ADP, Sep 2026](https://fantasyfootballcalculator.com/adp/ppr/10-team/all)).
Standard 10-team play: QB1 at rounds 10-11 + insurance QB2 at 12-13
(Matt Schauf, DraftSharks, [Sep 2, 2026](https://www.draftsharks.com/article/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-guide/10-team-ppr)).
VOR math: after Allen, the next QB (Hurts, VOR 27.3) "is barely above
the WR30 in value. The position is simply too deep to justify high draft
capital in single-QB formats" ([YAFSB, Mar 11, 2026](https://yafsb.com/content/vor-value-over-replacement-fantasy-football/)).
10-team splurge rule: a 10-team manager "can afford to go after a top QB
and/or TE rather than stacking WRs and RBs... But 'top' is the key"
(Schauf, [Sep 2, 2026](https://www.draftsharks.com/article/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-guide/10-team-ppr)).

## TE (10-team)

Scarcity case: top-3 TEs averaged 15.5-15.8 PPR in 2025 with "a full
two-point gap between them and everyone else" ([PFF, Jan 27, 2025](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-rankings-2025-tight-end-tiers)).
Only 10 TE slots exist league-wide, so TE3 is the last "trustable" TE by
ADP; timing rule: address TE in the middle, after the RB/WR core, before
QB1 ([DraftSharks, Sep 2, 2026](https://www.draftsharks.com/article/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-guide/10-team-ppr)).
A TE in FLEX also consumes the TE slot, doubling its weekly value - "one
elite/mid TE + a late TE insurance pick is the norm" ([Fantasy Life, mid-2025](https://www.fantasylife.com/articles/fantasy/fantasy-football-draft-strategies-for-2025-from-zero-rb-to-hero)).

## K and DEF

Correlation between ADP and final rank is near zero: Spearman 0.042 for
kickers, 0.125 for D/ST in 2024 - below the ~0.2 "barely better than dice"
floor (Scott Reida, FFinsights, [Aug 15, 2025](https://www.ffinsights.com/p/the-paradox-of-kickers-and-defenses)).
Rule: final two rounds (13-14 here) for both, never a starter slot;
stream afterward. Market confirmation: first DEF ADP 82.6 (round 9),
first K 120.9 (round 12+), 10-team PPR 2026 ([FFC](https://fantasyfootballcalculator.com/adp/ppr/10-team/all)).
The top drafted D/ST finished ninth or worse the next season in 7 of the
past 9 years (Mike Clay, ESPN, [Jun 3, 2026](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/48937876/2026-fantasy-football-draft-secrets-tips-nfl)).
