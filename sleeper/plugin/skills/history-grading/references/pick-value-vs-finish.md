# Grading a single draft pick

Source: `research-r8-history-grading-v2.md` §1(a).

## Freeze the price, compare to finish

Price a pick at its ADP slot at draft time; grade it against the player's
end-of-season *positional* finish, using PPG rather than total points
because "injuries aren't very predictable" ([PFF, Jan 13, 2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-season-in-review-what-nathan-jahnke-got-right-and-wrong-2025)).
A pick whose rank matched its ADP is neither right nor wrong.

## Healthy-play filter first

Apply a healthy-play filter before calling a bust a draft error: a player
who "played in the large majority of games" and still underperformed is
the analyzer's case; injury-driven misses are excluded from draft-error
accounting ([PFF, Dec 30, 2025](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-pff-2025-fantasy-football-awards)).

## Opportunity cost (Draft Leverage Differential)

Grade the pick against what would have been available at the manager's
*next* pick, not against one other player — the historical value of the
slot taken minus the historical value of the slot expected at the next
pick ([FantasyLabs, Jun 28, 2018](https://www.fantasylabs.com/articles/fantasy-football-rankings-draft-leverage-differential-historical-data/)).
This source's slot values predate 2024-26 boards — flag as an evergreen
methodology, not current pricing, when citing it.

## Replacement level: three conventions, print which one

No single convention is standard. Three exist:

| Convention | 12-team baseline | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench / 12th man | QB18, RB30, WR42, TE18 | [PFF, May 27, 2015](https://www.pff.com/news/the-value-of-replacement-level) |
| Last starter | QB12, RB36, WR48, TE12 | [YAFSB, Mar 11, 2026](https://yafsb.com/content/vor-value-over-replacement-fantasy-football/) |
| Post-draft waiver wire, floored at 0 | — | [FantasyPros, updated Mar 30, 2020](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005868747-What-is-value-based-drafting-What-do-player-draft-values-mean-VORP-VONA-VOLS-VBD) |

For this 10-team league the last-starter convention maps to roughly
QB10 / ~RB30 / ~WR30 / TE10 (researcher-derived, not a published table).
Whichever convention is used, the output must print which one.

## Slot-quality check

Compare the manager's draft slot to the best slot before blaming
individual picks: in a 10-team PPR league, the No. 8 spot captures 14.1%
fewer 3D Value points than the No. 3 pick through six rounds
([DraftSharks, Aug 26, 2026](https://www.draftsharks.com/article/best-draft-position-fantasy-football-10-team)).

## Off-market picks

An off-market pick is defensible only with a stated structural reason;
the decision test is next-pick availability — "Can I get the player I
want at my next pick? If the answer isn't confidently 'yes,' then it's
not a reach" ([FantasyPros, May 2019](https://www.fantasypros.com/2019/05/when-is-it-okay-to-reach-on-draft-day-fantasy-football/)).

## Gap

No peer-reviewed study of fantasy draft grading exists in the 2024-2026
literature. Cite the named-analyst anchors above; do not invent a
universal grading formula.
