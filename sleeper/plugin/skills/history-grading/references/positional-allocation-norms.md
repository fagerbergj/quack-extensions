# Positional allocation norms (10-team PPR)

Source: `research-r8-history-grading-v2.md` §1(b), §2.

## Target shape

Roughly 4-5 RB, 4-5 WR, 1-2 QB, 1-2 TE, K/DEF in the final rounds
([DraftSharks, Aug 22, 2026](https://www.draftsharks.com/article/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-guide/10-team-ppr)).
Round map for a 10-team board: Round 1 RB or WR, Round 2 WR, Round 3
QB/RB/TE, Round 4 WR/RB/top TE ([PFF, Jun 30, 2025](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-perfect-10-team-2025-draft-strategy-round-by-round)).

## RB-heavy vs. Zero RB — manager chooses

This is a live disagreement, not a rule: 2024's top-4 RBs all scored
336+ PPR while RB5-21 sat in a 200-300 band, a real cliff ([FantasyLabs, Aug 18, 2025](https://www.fantasylabs.com/articles/hero-rb-explained-top-fantasy-football-targets-for-2025/));
DraftSharks' 2026 data says the optimal 10-team PPR build is Zero RB,
340.3 vs. 319.9 3D Value by ADP ([DraftSharks, Aug 26, 2026](https://www.draftsharks.com/article/best-draft-position-fantasy-football-10-team)).
The bridge: elite RBs are the most over-projected tier (RB1-5 miss by
~55 points) yet the most durable (85.0% of available games over five
seasons) ([FFA, Jul 2025](https://fantasyfootballanalytics.net/2025/07/fantasy-football-projections-exploring-positional-bias-in-projections.html); [Fantasy Index, Aug 24, 2026](https://fantasyindex.com/2026/08/24/factoid/risky-business)).
Hold the manager to whichever shape they declared; grade RBs taken
against that declared shape, not against the other camp's rule.

## QB timing

QB1→QB12 gap was 6.8 PPG in 2024 vs. 9.5 (RB1→RB24) and 11.3 (WR1→WR36)
— "taking a quarterback that early is usually a mistake"
([Footballguys, Jun 6, 2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-fantasy-draft-strategy-guide-quarterbacks)).
QB6-10 is the single most over-projected tier: ~42-point average miss
vs. preseason projections over six seasons, ballooning to ~80 in the
last three ([FFA, Jul 2025](https://fantasyfootballanalytics.net/2025/07/fantasy-football-projections-exploring-positional-bias-in-projections.html)).
Market data as tiebreaker (2026 10-team board): QB1 in Round 3 (Allen
25.5), QB2-3 at the Round 4/5 turn, QB4+ in Rounds 8+, QB10 ≈ Round 13
([FFC 10-team 2026 ADP](https://fantasyfootballcalculator.com/adp/ppr/10-team/all)).
Never QB6-10 — both camps agree on this.

## TE

The cliff is flatter than its reputation: TE1→TE12 gap was 5.6 PPR PPG
in 2024, the lowest of any position, with the top-3 within 30 points
total ([Footballguys, Jun 27, 2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-fantasy-draft-strategy-guide-tight-ends)).
Either take elite or punt: TE1-3 picks had a 10% bust rate and +8.6
value vs. 26.7% and -38.8 for TE4-12, 12-team PPR 2015-2024
([Fantasy Football Blueprint, Jul 28, 2026](https://www.fantasyfootballblueprint.com/2026/07/28/the-elite-te-conditional/)).

## K / DEF

Orthodox: exactly one K, final round only ([FantasyPros, Jun 2026](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/06/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-kickers-2026/)).
DEF: stream it by matchup rather than invest heavily — weekly matchup
beats team identity ([FantasyPros, Jun 2026](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/06/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-streaming-defenses-2026/); [ESPN, Sep 2, 2026](https://www.espn.com.au/fantasy/football/story/_/id/49798496/2026-fantasy-football-d-st-road-map-early-season)).

## Format warning

Never use a 12-team ADP, half-PPR consistency band, or a 12-team
lineup-efficiency norm as this league's threshold without an explicit
10-team/PPR adaptation flag.
