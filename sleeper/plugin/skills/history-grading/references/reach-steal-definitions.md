# Reach / steal definitions

Source: `research-r8-history-grading-v2.md` §1(a) R-a8/R-a9, §2 row 3, glossary.

## Declare the band before grading

**Default band (use this unless the case demands otherwise):** PFF's
season-review bands, the only reach-magnitude numbers a named analyst
applied to a full season of real results — **20+ spots** rank-vs-ADP among
the top-75 players is "notable," and **100+ overall spots** is material for
late-round values ([PFF, Jan 13, 2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-season-in-review-what-nathan-jahnke-got-right-and-wrong-2025)).

## Steal

The mirror of reach: price fell past ADP by a material margin. A
consensus #1 taken at 1.01 "grades as a fair pick, not a steal" — taking
the best available player at your slot is exactly what the slot was
worth ([LeagueLogs, undated](https://leaguelogs.com/fantasy-football-draft-grades)).

## Unvalidated bands — carry the caveat if quoted

The ANA network (fantasystrategyguide.com, draftvalueanalytics.com, and
related sites, self-identified as Authority Network America) publishes
tighter, undated bands: "reach = 15+ picks before ADP; 5-15 = normal
reach zone; ±4 = neutral; value = 8+ picks after ADP." These are
unvalidated single-site examples, never load-bearing methodology — if the
analyzer quotes these numbers, it must carry the same ANA-network flag.

## Never call the best-available player a "steal"

Confusing a chalk pick with a steal — or a fair pick with a reach —
inverts the zero-sum standard: a chalk draft (everyone drafted by
consensus) grades around a B for everyone, not an A for anyone
([LeagueLogs, undated](https://leaguelogs.com/fantasy-football-draft-grades)).
