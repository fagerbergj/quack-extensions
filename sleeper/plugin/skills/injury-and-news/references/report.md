# R5: Injury & NFL News Reliability for Fantasy Decisions — Research Report

*Scope: 2025–2026 seasons (plus the 2016 policy revision and sports-medicine literature that ground the numbers). Compiled 2026-09-18. All claims carry an inline URL and the most specific date the source notes provide; items the researchers could not verify are flagged as such rather than smoothed over.*

## Decision rules & numeric thresholds

1. **Treat final (Friday) practice participation as the single best weekly predictor of "will play."** Across a 2,000+ injury database (2017–2023): Full participant ≈ **86%**, Limited ≈ **71%**, DNP ≈ **29%** chance to play; injury-stratified: concussion LP ≈ **57%**, hamstring-strain DNP ≈ **20%**, knee LP ≈ **81%**. Operational reading: a Friday DNP ≈ treat as a game-time decision leaning out, **not** an automatic bench (a DNP player still suits up ~1 in 3). Source: [Footballguys, "Chance to Play: Practice Participation," published 09/01/2024](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2024-injury-index-chance-to-play-practice-participation); a 2025-updated edition with the 2024 season added exists but its table is paywalled ([Footballguys, 09/01/2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-practice-participation-and-chance-to-play)) — numbers above are from the published 2024 edition.

2. **Handle designations in tiers, and always with a named contingency:** Questionable → start if the replacement is clearly worse; Doubtful → plan as out and have the backup ready before Sunday; Out/IR → drop or stream. The published rule of thumb: *"The worst start/sit mistakes aren't wrong decisions — they're no decisions. If a player is questionable and you don't have a contingency plan, that's the failure."* ([Waiver Wizard, "Start/Sit Decisions," byline 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/start-sit-decisions))

3. **Do not treat "Questionable" as a coin flip or a guarantee.** Post-2016 play rates: **70.7%** of final-report Q players played ([Footballguys, 09/13/2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-chance-to-play-questionable-vs-doubtful)); ≈**73%** ([PFF data study, 06/14/2021](https://www.pff.com/news/nfl-pff-data-study-war-adjusted-injuries-lost); independent confirmation that the real rate jumped to ~73% after "Probable" was eliminated — [NFL Injury Analytics, 11/06/2016](https://nflinjuryanalyticscom.wordpress.com/2016/11/06/the-effects-of-eliminating-probable-from-the-nfl-injury-report/)); 50–70% per a 2026 practice guide ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)). The league eliminated "Probable" before 2016 because roughly **95%** of such players ended up playing ([ESPN, Kevin Seifert, 08/21/2016](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/17361111/nfl-streamlines-injured-players-game-status-removes-probable-designation)). Pre-2016 numbers are **not comparable**: back when Q meant 50/50, Q players played ~90% and "doubtful" was defined as 75% out ([USA Today injury-report audit, 11/22/2007](http://usatoday30.usatoday.com/sports/football/nfl/2007-11-22-injury-report-cover_N.htm)).

4. **The inactive list, filed 90 minutes before kickoff, is the only definitive game-day document.** NFL Constitution Article XVII §17.3 requires each club to establish its 45-player Active List by notifying the Referee of its Inactive List "one hour and 30 minutes prior to kickoff" (verbatim bylaw text quoted in [NFL.com, May 21, 2023](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-owners-pass-proposal-to-allow-teams-to-have-third-qb-active-on-game-days-wit); full 2023 bylaw-proposal record at [readkong, May 2023](https://www.readkong.com/page/2023-playing-rules-bylaw-and-resolution-proposals-6778580); summarized in [JetNation, 05/23/2023](https://www.jetnation.com/2023/05/23/nfls-emergency-quarterback-rule/)). **No credible source states a 75-minute NFL inactives rule** — "75 minutes" figures are soccer/college-officiating rules. In practice the list "should begin coming in approximately 90 minutes before the scheduled kickoff times: around 11:30 a.m. ET for the early games" ([ESPN fantasy inactives hub, 11/03/2024](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/page/FFSundayInactives-41109343/nfl-fantasy-football-espn-injured-inactives-watchlist-week-9-2024-november-3); same framing in ESPN's [Week 3 2025 page](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/page/FFSundayInactives-46311253/nfl-fantasy-football-espn-injured-inactives-watchlist-week-3-2025-september-21)).

5. **"Game-time decision" (GTD) is not an official league designation.** The league only issues Out / Doubtful / Questionable (no line = presumed active); GTD is media/fantasy shorthand for a Questionable whose answer arrives with inactives. A codified loophole confirms it: if a player is still being worked out on game day, the club **is not required to update its Game Status Report** if it decides he won't play — so a Friday Questionable can sit untouched until the 90-minute deadline ([NBC Sports ProFootballTalk](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill/news/game-time-decision-loophole-helps-teams-avoid-having-to-downgrade-players-from-questionable) — note: the source notes date this article variously late Jan 2023 and 09/14/2024; treat the exact date as unverified, the mechanism is corroborated across multiple outlets). **No league-level "GTD resolution rate" statistic exists** — the only hard percentage tied to an official league statement is the ~95% of "probable" players who played (2016). Do not invent a rate.

6. **After Friday 4:00 p.m. ET, only Saturday roster moves and the 90-minute inactive list change your Sunday decision** ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)). The official filing cadence for a Sunday game: **Practice Reports by 4:00 p.m. ET (or as soon as practice ends) Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday**, and a **Game Status Report by 4:00 p.m. ET Friday**; "an update must be reported if there is any change in a player's condition after the initial Game Status Report is filed" ([PFWA NFL Calendar, 2026 edition](https://www.profootballwriters.org/nfl-calendar/); identical language on [NFL.com 2026–27 important dates](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-27-national-football-league-important-dates); also on [NFL Football Operations](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-important-dates)). Note there is **no standing Tuesday league injury report** under current rules. Corollary: the Wed→Thu→Fri practice *trend* is what tells you which way a Questionable breaks ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)), and anything questionable should be re-decided **Sunday morning** — "a Wednesday lineup is a guess made three days before the information arrives" ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/start-sit-decisions)).

7. **Know your lineup-lock timing relative to inactives.** ESPN's default lock is per-player at the scheduled game time, but leagues can be set to a weekly/first-game lock ([ESPN Fan Support, accessed 09/18/2026](https://support.espn.com/hc/en-us/articles/360054748151-Lineup-and-Roster-Lock-Times)); Yahoo defaults likewise to the player's own game start ([Yahoo Help, accessed 09/18/2026](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/fantasy-football/transaction-lineup-deadlines-yahoo-fantasy-sln6775.html)). Concretely: the first Sunday kickoff is 1:00 p.m. ET → inactives post ~12:30 p.m. → on per-game lock you have only **30 minutes** between inactives and lock, and on weekly lock your roster is frozen **before** that game's inactives ([Fantasy Injury Report Authority, undated — SEO-content flag](https://fantasyinjuryreportauthority.com/late-injury-news-fantasy-lineup-decisions)). This is the structural reason a contingency plan, not a locked best guess, is the unit of a correct lineup decision.

8. **Waiver timing: act within hours of an Out/IR designation** — "the best backup will be claimed within hours." Canonical 2025 example: after Chuba Hubbard went down, Rico Dowdle (owned in only ~5% of leagues) was claimed fast and finished a top-20 per-game back ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports); [Yahoo, "All-Waiver-Wire Team of 2025"](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/fantasy-football-all-waiver-wire-team-michael-wilson-matthew-stafford-among-best-pickups-of-2025-season-182434653.html)). Saturday roster elevations/signings are the tell that a team has privately given up on a player ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)).

9. **FAAB on injury replacements — use published proportions, not vibes.** The cleanest published convention is bidding **as a percentage of remaining bank**, which RotoWire attaches to every pickup because league contexts differ ([RotoWire, "Top Fantasy Football Waiver Wire Pickups: NFL Week 2," 2026 season, updated 09/14/2026](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/fantasy-football-waiver-wire-pickups-for-week-2-134360)). A full published formula: **Bid = min(0.40 × remaining bank, (remaining bank ÷ weeks left) × tier × competition × need)**, with a **hard 40%-of-bank cap** per bid, **$1–3 minimum bids** for speculative handcuffs, and explicit advice to **preserve budget through the fantasy playoffs (weeks 14–16)** ([Calculator Collection, FAAB Bid Calculator, last updated Sept 2026](https://www.calculatorcollection.org/en/calculators/fantasy-sports/fantasy-waiver-priority-calculator/)). Bid on **usage** (snap share, target share, route participation), not projected points ([Pro Football Network, undated guide](https://www.profootballnetwork.com/fantasy-football/burn-learn-10-fantasy-football-waiver-wire-faab-mistakes-youll-never-make-again/)); do not assume the longest possible absence for a temporary fill-in ([SharkSnip, 2026](https://sharksnip.com/blog/fantasy-waiver-wire-faab-strategy)).

10. **Injury-type windows — play-through vs. shutdown.** **Play-through (soft tissue):** hamstring — mean **2.4 ± 2.1 weeks** on the NFL injury report across 2,101 injuries, 2008–2020, with a **33% re-injury rate** ([peer-reviewed, OJSM, published 12/06/2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11624564/)); skill-position hamstring strains miss a **mean 1.4 games (median 1, mode 0)** — 33% miss zero games ([Fantasy Points, 2021](https://web.dev.fantasypoints.com/nfl/articles/2021/what-to-expect-hamstring-strains)). Ankle: peer-reviewed studies document material time loss with **high-ankle/syndesmosis sprains worse than lateral** ([OJSM, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1177/23259671221101056); [comparison study, 2013](https://doi.org/10.3928/01477447-20131021-18); [fantasy-outcome study, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jor.2024.03.030)) — **no single clean "mean weeks" ankle figure could be verified** from open text; treat "several weeks, longer for high ankle" as the literature-supported range. **Shutdown:** ACL — returning WRs average **−17.9% target share and −26.6% yards per route** the season after reconstruction ([Footballguys, 07/05/2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-digging-into-wrs-returning-from-acl-reconstruction)); post-ACL skill-position production falls roughly a third ([Vanderbilt, 11/30/2006](https://news.vanderbilt.edu/2006/11/30/performance-of-wide-receivers-running-backs-post-acl-injury-falls-by-one-third-58809/)); return-to-play rates are lower than the classic 79% figure ([Manoharan et al., OJSM, 01/21/2021](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2325967120974743)). **Concussion:** governed by the graduated Return-to-Participation Protocol with **no fixed minimum** — progression is physician-judged ([NFL Return-to-Participation Protocol, current](https://www.nfl.com/playerhealthandsafety/resources/fact-sheets/nfl-return-to-participation-protocol)). **IR (Reserve/Injured List):** out a **minimum of 4 games**, up to 8 return designations per team per season ([PlaybookWire, 08/03/2026](https://playbookwire.com/nfl-injured-reserve-rules-how-ir-works-in-2026/); 2026 rule tweaks in [The Athletic, 04/02/2026](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7165454/2026/04/02/nfl-roster-rule-changes-2026/)). Decision rule: IR move ≈ out ≥4 games; a non-designated IR move before cutdowns ≈ season-ending — the trigger for full FAAB commitment, not a speculative bid ([PlaybookWire, 08/03/2026](https://playbookwire.com/nfl-injured-reserve-rules-how-ir-works-in-2026/)).

11. **Source-weighting rule: the original observer at practice wins conflicts.** Published hierarchy: (a) direct practice observation by a credentialed on-site reporter, (b) locker-room/one-on-one quotes, (c) second-hand/press-box accounts, (d) anonymous "sources"; the team's own announcement is "definitive but arrives last," and aggregator accounts are "the slowest useful tier" ([Fantasy News Authority, undated](https://fantasynewsauthority.com/beat-reporters-fantasy-news-value/); [Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)). **Caveat preserved:** the minute-level lag figures (beat reporters 10–30 min ahead of designations; aggregators 15–60 min delay) are directional estimates from undated pages, not dated measurements ([OddsProbe, undated](https://www.oddsprobe.com/reporters); [Fantasy Injury Report Authority, undated](https://fantasyinjuryreportauthority.com/beat-reporter-injury-intel-fantasy)); the dated, verifiable anchors are the NFL's 4:00 p.m. filing deadlines and the ~90-minute inactives window.

12. **Treat team-published depth charts as context, and reps as the story.** Teams publish charts weekly and their own copy flags them as **"unofficial"** projections ([Falcons.com, 09/15/2026](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/atlanta-falcons-depth-chart-carolina-panthers)); the live 2026 case study shows the chart and the actual reps directly disagreeing within 24 hours — Tua atop the Falcons chart Tuesday, then Michael Penix Jr. splitting first-team snaps with Cooper Rush Wednesday ([AJC, Daniel Flick, 09/16/2026](https://www.ajc.com/sports/2026/09/atlanta-falcons-qb-michael-penix-jr-says-he-may-be-ready-against-panthers/); [Falcons Wire, 09/15/2026](https://thefalconswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/falcons/2026/09/15/atlanta-falcons-depth-chart-panthers/91777839007/)). Caveat: reps in early weeks are a staff *plan* that injury/matchup/game script can override, and no quantified "reps lead production by N weeks" claim could be verified — the published analytics framing is qualitative ([Fantasy Injury Report Authority, undated](https://fantasyinjuryreportauthority.com/beat-reporter-injury-intel-fantasy/)).

13. **Week 1 is the least data-rich week of the season.** Between preseason's end (camp final day Sunday 09/06/2026; rosters cut to 53 by 4:00 p.m. ET Sunday 08/30/2026 — [PFWA 2026 calendar](https://www.profootballwriters.org/nfl-calendar/); [NFL.com 2026–27 dates](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-27-national-football-league-important-dates)) and the first practice week there is effectively no reportable data ([Go Birds Daily, 09/03/2026](https://www.podscan.fm/podcasts/go-birds/episodes/go-birds-daily-sept-3rd-howie-roseman-gives-strongest-endorsement-yet-of-jalen-hurts-future-with-eagles-1); [steelers.com, 09/08/2026](https://www.steelers.com/news/work-begins-on-the-falcons)). Consequence: Week 1's first practice report arrives only on the first full practice day (Wed 09/09/2026 for 09/13 games), so at most three data points exist, and a Friday designation can be a pure estimate — e.g., Pittsburgh's walkthrough-only week produced a Friday "Questionable" that "came as an estimate" ([Steelers Depot, 09/09–11/2026](https://steelersdepot.com/2026/09/steelers-week-1-friday-injury-report-joey-porter-jr-ends-week-with-uncertain-status/); [KOMO News, 09/08/2026](https://komonews.com/sports/nikos-sports-zone/seattle-seahawks-head-coach-mike-macdonald-national-football-league-nfl-final-practice-new-england-patriots-week-1-labor-day-emmanwori)). The inactives list remains the only definitive anchor.

14. **Practice-report lines are not availability signals** — the policy requires listing *any* noteworthy injury even for a player who takes full reps and is certain to play ("especially true of key players"), so a Full participant can carry a report line all season ([2016 Personnel (Injury) Report Policy, 06/07/2016, PDF](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/sportsarchive-documents/prod/681949c029f45/06-07-16-2016-injury-report-policy.pdf); [Sporting News explainer, May 2024](https://www.sportingnews.com/au/nfl/news/nfl-injury-report-rules-penalties-violations-2024/05ab0439361a73dd047fec2f); the Bengals/Joe Burrow investigation centered on exactly this provision, [Sporting News, 2021](https://www.sportingnews.com/au/nfl/news/nfl-investigation-bengals-injury-report-policy-joe-burrow/b110069f47a315cbe60f2196)).

## Where experts disagree

1. **Act on a single beat-reporter injury tweet before team/official confirmation?**
   - **Pro-act:** beat reporters post immediately after the open-practice window — the cited 10–30 minute head start (undated industry estimate, [OddsProbe](https://www.oddsprobe.com/reporters)) — and in a rolling-waiver/FAAB league the pickup window closes fast, so waiting "risks a waiver wire emptied by faster-moving managers" ([Fantasy Injury Report Authority, undated](https://fantasyinjuryreportauthority.com/late-injury-news-fantasy-lineup-decisions)).
   - **Con (verify first):** by the time a manager sees a downstream alert, "the original claim may already have been reframed 3 or 4 times, each step adding interpretive language not present in the source tweet" ([Fantasy News Authority, undated](https://fantasynewsauthority.com/fantasy-news-twitter-social-media)); and even a top name can mislead in the first minutes — Adam Schefter's Nick Chubb injury tweet on 09/18/2023 opened with Chubb's *2015* knee injury, drawing immediate, heavy criticism ([The Comeback, 09/18/2023](https://thecomeback.com/nfl/nick-chubb-adam-schefter-injury-tweet.html)).
   - **Published synthesis:** the original beat tweet is safe for *waiver pickups*; for *starting a player you believe is out*, wait for the 90-minute inactive list ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)).
2. **Ride a GTD starter (with contingency) or resolve early / floor-first?**
   - **Ride, with a named backup:** FantasyPros' Derek Brown on a WR with an 80% model probability: "If he plays, you play him. Just make sure you have a backup option…" ([FantasyPros player note, 10/19/2025](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/notes/409180/emeka-egbuka-2025-week-7-outlook.php)); PFF's start/sit desk treats GTD flags as an adjustment on an otherwise-tied comparison, not an automatic bench ([PFF, Nathan Jahnke, 09/17/2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-week-2-start-em-sit-em)); Footballguys publishes a "Gameday Injury Expectations" product for exactly this ([09/12/2026](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2026-gameday-injury-expectations-week01)).
   - **Floor-first:** a coin-flip Questionable with an equally good replacement "is worth resolving by 11 a.m. Sunday, not 1:04 p.m.," and in playoff weeks the floor dominates — a loss ends the season ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/start-sit-decisions); restated in [Fantasy Injury Report Authority, undated](https://fantasyinjuryreportauthority.com/questionable-tag-fantasy-football-strategy)). For a 10-team league with 6 playoff teams from Week 15, the conservative side has the better math in ~4–6 high-stakes GTD weeks.
3. **Does "Limited practice" auto-start or auto-sit?**
   - **Auto-start (base rate):** LP ≈ 71% overall, 81–92% for knees — the practice *trend*, not the tag, drives the call ([Footballguys, 09/01/2024](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2024-injury-index-chance-to-play-practice-participation); [Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)).
   - **Type-dependent downgrade:** the *same* Footballguys table shows LP is only 57% for concussion and 62% for hamstring strain — for those families LP should at least force a contingency or auto-sit. The data supports stratifying over one universal LP number.
4. **Aggressive vs. conservative FAAB on injury replacements.**
   - **Aggressive/immediate:** "act immediately. Don't wait for Saturday morning to check waivers" — the 2025 Dowdle case is the proof ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)).
   - **Conservative/role-priced:** no universal bid table; price the confirmed role and do not "assume the longest possible absence" for a temporary fill-in ([SharkSnip, 2026](https://sharksnip.com/blog/fantasy-waiver-wire-faab-strategy)).
   - **Numeric middle ground:** RotoWire's %-of-bank convention ([updated 09/14/2026](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/fantasy-football-waiver-wire-pickups-for-week-2-134360)) and the 40%-of-bank cap with a $1–3 floor ([Calculator Collection, Sept 2026](https://www.calculatorcollection.org/en/calculators/fantasy-sports/fantasy-waiver-priority-calculator/)).
5. **Are Friday Q/D designations worth anything?**
   - **Yes, as a floor:** they are the only mandatory, league-enforced disclosure, and the final report + final practice participation carry the strongest published predictive numbers ([NFL Football Operations 2026–27 calendar](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-important-dates); [Footballguys, 09/13/2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-chance-to-play-questionable-vs-doubtful)).
   - **No, as a ceiling:** teams "do not use the Questionable tag correctly"; "Probable" was removed precisely because ~95% of such players played anyway ([ESPN, 08/21/2016](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/17361111/nfl-streamlines-injured-players-game-status-removes-probable-designation)); and in the McCaffrey case a fully compliant Questionable was still a bench decision made 90 minutes out, with the NFL finding no policy violation (see date conflict below).
6. **Data conflict to surface, not hide — "Doubtful" base rate:** ≈**1%** in PFF's 2021 study ([06/14/2021](https://www.pff.com/news/nfl-pff-data-study-war-adjusted-injuries-lost)); **6.9%** in Footballguys' 2025 edition ([09/13/2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-chance-to-play-questionable-vs-doubtful)); **20–25%** in Waiver Wizard's 2026 guide ([07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)). The defensible assistant behavior, regardless of which estimate is trusted: plan as out, never assume certain, keep a contingency.
7. **Depth chart vs. practice reps as the source of truth for a role change.** The team-published chart is the staff's *stated, projected* plan (self-labeled "unofficial"); reps are the staff's *working* plan. The Falcons' Week 2 2026 week is the documented divergence — Tagovailoa atop the chart Tuesday 09/15, Penix/Rush splitting first-team snaps Wednesday 09/16 ([Falcons.com, 09/15/2026](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/atlanta-falcons-depth-chart-carolina-panthers); [AJC, 09/16/2026](https://www.ajc.com/sports/2026/09/atlanta-falcons-qb-michael-penix-jr-says-he-may-be-ready-against-panthers/)).
8. **"Doctor" accounts for injury timelines.** Some fantasy users follow armchair-diagnosis accounts for medical color; but the documented case is Dr. Jesse Morse diagnosing Christian Watson with a "high ankle sprain" from video, with the player's father correcting him (X-ray/MRI showed a *medial* sprain) and Morse still insisting ([Awful Announcing, 10/11/2024](https://awfulannouncing.com/nfl/christian-watson-social-media-injury-expert.html)) — and even major outlets have moved to calibrated ML play-probability tools rather than news-guessing ([FantasyPros blog, "Are They Playing?" undated](https://blog.fantasypros.com/are-they-playing-predict-injury-outcomes-with-confidence/)). The documented weight: video-based "diagnosis" is a false signal, not a source.
9. **Date conflict in the source notes (flagged, unresolved):** the canonical "Q all week → ruled out at inactives, league finds no violation" McCaffrey case is cited as **September 2024** (statement relayed via NFL Network, [NBC Sports Bay Area, 09/13/2024](https://www.nbcsportsbayarea.com/nfl/san-francisco-49ers/christian-mccaffrey-sratch-league-policy-violation/1781706/)) and as **September 2025** ([CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/christian-mccaffrey-controversy-nfl-clears-49ers-of-any-wrongdoing-in-terms-of-reporting-star-rbs-injury/); [Yahoo on the loophole](https://sports.yahoo.com/game-time-decision-loophole-helps-230439611.html)); the ProFootballTalk loophole article is dated late Jan 2023 in one note and 09/14/2024 in two others (same URL). The article bodies were not fully retrievable; the facts are corroborated verbatim across six outlets' search results. The *mechanism* (a Friday Questionable can be scratched at inactives without penalty) is consistent across all accounts.
10. **Consensus exists (do not manufacture a disagreement):** (a) the inactives deadline is **90 minutes**, per bylaws — no credible source states 75 minutes for the NFL ([NFL.com, 05/21/2023](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-owners-pass-proposal-to-allow-teams-to-have-third-qb-active-on-game-days-wit)); (b) the practice-report **trend** is more predictive than the designation itself ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports); [Fantasy Injury Report Authority, undated](https://fantasyinjuryreportauthority.com/injury-report-accuracy-and-reliability)); (c) the original source outranks the aggregator, and the reporter physically at practice wins a conflict ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)); (d) a designation is the output of a legal minimum-disclosure obligation, not a medical bulletin ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports); policy text in the [2016 Personnel (Injury) Report Policy](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/sportsarchive-documents/prod/681949c029f45/06-07-16-2016-injury-report-policy.pdf)).

**Additional preserved caveats:** named-reporter tier lists are undated follow-lists/SEO guide pages, not a dated, established outlet's published ranking of *injury-source* accuracy ([ReadTheBench, undated](https://www.readthebench.com/nfl-news-reporters-writers/); [OddsProbe, undated](https://www.oddsprobe.com/reporters)); the Waiver Wizard pages carry no byline date in some renderings — the 07/06/2026 date comes from the article's own byline line in one retrieval ([Waiver Wizard](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)); the exact current policy text (including any NFLPA-agreed wording that permits vague labels like "concussion protocol," seen live in [Packers Wire, 09/16/2026](https://packerswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/packers/2026/09/16/packers-injury-report-lukas-van-ness-javon-hargrave-in-concussion-protocol/91798202007/)) could not be retrieved as a clean public page — treat specific "the policy permits phrase X" claims as unverified; the full bylaws PDFs are binary-gated, with §17.3 text quoted by three independent secondary carriers.

## What bad advice looks like

1. **Treating "Questionable" (or "expected to play") as guaranteed.** Base rate is ~71–73% post-2016, not 100% — and team Q play rates vary hugely ([Footballguys, 09/13/2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-chance-to-play-questionable-vs-doubtful); team-level spread in [Harvard Sports Analysis Collective, 2013](https://harvardsportsanalysis.org/2013/11/inaccuracies-in-the-injury-report-across-the-nfl/) — e.g., Buffalo ~98% of Probables played vs. New Orleans ~36% of Questionables in 2012). Documented damage case: McCaffrey Questionable all week, scratched at inactives, no policy violation ([CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/christian-mccaffrey-controversy-nfl-clears-49ers-of-any-wrongdoing-in-terms-of-reporting-star-rbs-injury/)).
2. **Treating "Doubtful" as a coin flip — or symmetrically, as 100% out with no plan.** Estimates span 1%–25% by study ([PFF, 06/14/2021](https://www.pff.com/news/nfl-pff-data-study-war-adjusted-injuries-lost) vs. [Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)); the defensible move is "plan as out, keep a contingency" — advice that does neither fails.
3. **Carrying last season's injury (or last season's performance) into this week's status.** Post-ACL value is structurally different — WRs average −17.9% target share and −26.6% yards per route the year after reconstruction ([Footballguys, 07/05/2025](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2025-digging-into-wrs-returning-from-acl-reconstruction)) — but players fully recover within a season: Jonathon Brooks was LP early in 2026 and **full on Friday, removed from the report**, for Week 1 ([Footballguys Week 1 injury report, 09/2026](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2026-nfl-week-1-injury-report-fantasy-football-impact-for-every-game)). An answer claiming "Brooks is still ACL-limited" in Week 1 2026 fails.
4. **Citing an aggregator without the original source, or acting on aggregator lag.** Platforms re-post beat-reporter observations with a 15–60 minute delay and synthesize multiple reports, with interpretive drift at each step ([Fantasy Injury Report Authority, undated](https://fantasyinjuryreportauthority.com/beat-reporter-injury-intel-fantasy); [Fantasy News Authority, undated](https://fantasynewsauthority.com/fantasy-news-twitter-social-media)). A good answer names the reporter/outlet and states whether it's an observation or speculation — the standard in practice is per-player updates attributed to named reporters (e.g., [NBC Sports, Week 2 2026 report](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/news/2026-nfl-week-2-injury-report-a-j-brown-to-ir-updates-on-ladd-mcconkey-zay-flowers); [SI, 09/16/2026, quoting Schefter's post directly](https://www.si.com/onsi/fantasy/injuries/fantasy-football-injury-report-the-latest-updates-on-joe-burrow-zay-flowers-brock-bowers-and-all-the-top-stars)).
5. **Trusting a team-published depth chart as current.** Teams themselves label charts "unofficial" ([Falcons.com, 09/15/2026](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/atlanta-falcons-depth-chart-carolina-panthers)), and the documented case study shows the chart and the actual first-team reps disagreeing within 24 hours ([AJC, 09/16/2026](https://www.ajc.com/sports/2026/09/atlanta-falcons-qb-michael-penix-jr-says-he-may-be-ready-against-panthers/)). Even professional 32-team charts are "a snapshot… not a projection" ([PFF, 08/31/2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-2026-depth-charts-for-all-32-nfl-teams)); the published guidance is to treat team charts as "basically nothing actionable" on their own ([Fantasy Life, 08/10/2022, updated 06/18/2025](https://www.fantasylife.com/articles/newsletters/why-you-shouldnt-trust-depth-charts)).
6. **Conflating a participation level with a diagnosis (or a guarantee).** "Limited participation" is defined by the NFL purely as "less than 100% of normal reps" ([2016 Policy, 06/07/2016](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/sportsarchive-documents/prod/681949c029f45/06-07-16-2016-injury-report-policy.pdf); [ESPN, 08/21/2016](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/17361111/nfl-streamlines-injured-players-game-status-removes-probable-designation)); and an *FP* listing does not even guarantee the team's own intent — the Ravens were **fined $100,000** for listing Lamar Jackson as a full participant when he had only worked with the scout team ([NFL.com, 10/31/2025](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-fines-ravens-100k-incorrectly-listing-lamar-jackson-week-8-injury-report); [Reuters, 10/31/2025](https://www.reuters.com/sports/nfl-fines-ravens-100k-violating-injury-report-policy--flm-2025-10-31/)).
7. **Locking a lineup with no inactives contingency.** The explicit expert formulation: "If a player is questionable and you don't have a contingency plan, that's the failure. Always know who you're starting if your first option is ruled out." ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/start-sit-decisions)) The structural reason: lock timing vs. the 90-minute inactives means a lineup is finalized just after — or before — the only certain data point ([ESPN lock page, accessed 09/18/2026](https://support.espn.com/hc/en-us/articles/360054748151-Lineup-and-Roster-Lock-Times); [Fantasy Injury Report Authority, undated](https://fantasyinjuryreportauthority.com/late-injury-news-fantasy-lineup-decisions)).
8. **Recommending a FAAB spend with no dollar figure or percentage.** Every credible published column attaches a number — %-of-bank on every pickup ([RotoWire, updated 09/14/2026](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/fantasy-football-waiver-wire-pickups-for-week-2-134360)); a 40%-of-bank cap with a $1–3 floor for speculative adds ([Calculator Collection, Sept 2026](https://www.calculatorcollection.org/en/calculators/fantasy-sports/fantasy-waiver-priority-calculator/)). "Bid aggressively for him" with no figure is a fail; "bid ~15–20% of your bank if he's your must-have replacement, less if the starter is still trending toward playing" is the pattern to reward.
9. **Misquoting the inactives deadline (e.g., "75 minutes").** The bylaw-verified number is **90 minutes** (NFL Constitution Art. XVII §17.3, text quoted in [NFL.com, 05/21/2023](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-owners-pass-proposal-to-allow-teams-to-have-third-qb-active-on-game-days-wit); corroborated by ESPN's weekly inactives pages, e.g., [Week 3 2025](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/page/FFSundayInactives-46311253/nfl-fantasy-football-espn-injured-inactives-watchlist-week-3-2025-september-21)). No credible source states 75 minutes for the NFL — an assistant citing 75 fails.
10. **Reading coach-speak or vague "expected to play" as signal — or applying one universal percentage without team adjustment.** The live 2026 textbook: Pittsburgh's coach "wouldn't commit to Porter playing… he's repeated his line of Porter 'ramping up'" — while the practice data (Limited ×3) already resolved it ([Steelers Depot, 09/11/2026](https://steelersdepot.com/2026/09/steelers-week-1-friday-injury-report-joey-porter-jr-ends-week-with-uncertain-status/)); "a designation is the output of a legal obligation, and treating it as a medical bulletin is how managers end up surprised on Sunday" ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)). Team-level variability makes any unadjusted universal % unsound ([Harvard Sports Analysis Collective, 2013](https://harvardsportsanalysis.org/2013/11/inaccuracies-in-the-injury-report-across-the-nfl/); [NFL Injury Analytics, 11/06/2016](https://nflinjuryanalyticscom.wordpress.com/2016/11/06/the-effects-of-eliminating-probable-from-the-nfl-injury-report/)).

## Glossary

- **DNP / LP / FP (practice participation levels)** — Did Not Participate / Limited Participation (<100% of normal reps) / Full Participation (100% of normal reps); the three levels in team practice reports. Canonical terms: "DNP," "limited," "full participant" ([2016 Personnel (Injury) Report Policy, 06/07/2016](https://nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/sportsarchive-documents/prod/681949c029f45/06-07-16-2016-injury-report-policy.pdf)).
- **Practice report (team practice report)** — the team-filed document listing noteworthy injuries with participation level, due by 4:00 p.m. ET each practice day (Wed/Thu/Fri for Sunday games); an injury-tracking document, *not* an availability signal ([PFWA NFL Calendar, 2026](https://www.profootballwriters.org/nfl-calendar/); [Sporting News, May 2024](https://www.sportingnews.com/au/nfl/news/nfl-injury-report-rules-penalties-violations-2024/05ab0439361a73dd047fec2f)). Canonical term: "practice report."
- **Official injury report (Game Status Report)** — the league-mandated designation document (Out / Doubtful / Questionable) due 4:00 p.m. ET Friday for Sunday games; a player absent from it is presumed to play, and "Probable" has not existed since 2016 ([PFWA, 2026](https://www.profootballwriters.org/nfl-calendar/); [ESPN, 08/21/2016](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/17361111/nfl-streamlines-injured-players-game-status-removes-probable-designation)). Canonical terms: "Game Status Report" / "official injury report"; never "Tuesday report" (no such current report exists).
- **GTD (game-time decision)** — media/fantasy shorthand (not an NFL designation) for a Questionable whose answer arrives with the 90-minute inactive list ([NBC Sports ProFootballTalk](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill/news/game-time-decision-loophole-helps-teams-avoid-having-to-downgrade-players-from-questionable)). Canonical term: "GTD."
- **Day-to-day** — a handling convention for an undiagnosed or soft-tissue case whose status is re-evaluated at each report day (Wed/Thu/Fri) and each update through inactives, rather than assigned a fixed timeline (convention per the update mechanism in [PFWA, 2026](https://www.profootballwriters.org/nfl-calendar/) and the tier-handling in [Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/start-sit-decisions)). Canonical term: "day-to-day."
- **Inactives** — the club's inactive list, established with the Referee 90 minutes before kickoff (45-player active list of 53); the only binding, definitive statement of who plays ([NFL Constitution Art. XVII §17.3, quoted in NFL.com, 05/21/2023](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-owners-pass-proposal-to-allow-teams-to-have-third-qb-active-on-game-days-wit); [ESPN inactives hub, 11/03/2024](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/page/FFSundayInactives-41109343/nfl-fantasy-football-espn-injured-inactives-watchlist-week-9-2024-november-3)). Canonical term: "inactive list" / "inactives."
- **Lineup lock** — the moment a fantasy lineup becomes final; platforms default to per-player lock at the scheduled game time, with league-wide weekly/first-game lock as an option that can freeze the roster before the early games' inactives ([ESPN Fan Support, accessed 09/18/2026](https://support.espn.com/hc/en-us/articles/360054748151-Lineup-and-Roster-Lock-Times); [Yahoo Help, accessed 09/18/2026](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/fantasy-football/transaction-lineup-deadlines-yahoo-fantasy-sln6775.html)). Canonical term: "lineup lock."
- **Depth chart** — the team-published weekly projection of positional hierarchies; self-labeled "unofficial" by teams and explicitly "not a projection" by third parties, so treat it as a snapshot and prefer practice reps ([Falcons.com, 09/15/2026](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/atlanta-falcons-depth-chart-carolina-panthers); [PFF, 08/31/2026](https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-2026-depth-charts-for-all-32-nfl-teams)). Canonical term: "depth chart."
- **Reps** — practice repetitions; first-team snap allocation and the leading observable indicator of a player's intended role, more current than any published chart ([AJC, 09/16/2026](https://www.ajc.com/sports/2026/09/atlanta-falcons-qb-michael-penix-jr-says-he-may-be-ready-against-panthers/)). Canonical term: "reps" / "first-team reps."
- **Beat reporter** — the local team reporter with daily practice access and first-person observation; the most reliable tier for practice-granularity injury news, with national insiders (e.g., Schefter, Rapoport) outranking them only for league-wide, high-stakes news ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports); [ReadTheBench, undated](https://www.readthebench.com/nfl-news-reporters-writers/); [Fantasy News Authority, undated](https://fantasynewsauthority.com/beat-reporters-fantasy-news-value/)). Canonical term: "beat reporter."
- **Aggregator** — an account or platform feed that republishes original observations and official documents, typically with a 15–60 minute lag and interpretive drift; the slowest useful tier and never a primary citation ([Fantasy News Authority, undated](https://fantasynewsauthority.com/fantasy-news-twitter-social-media)). Canonical term: "aggregator."
- **FAAB (Free Agent Acquisition Budget)** — blind-bidding waiver system (commonly a $100 bank); published guidance expresses bids as a percentage of remaining bank, with a 40%-of-bank cap and $1–3 floors for speculative adds ([RotoWire, updated 09/14/2026](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/fantasy-football-waiver-wire-pickups-for-week-2-134360); [Calculator Collection, Sept 2026](https://www.calculatorcollection.org/en/calculators/fantasy-sports/fantasy-waiver-priority-calculator/); [Fantasy Waiver Wire, 2026](https://fantasywaiverwire.com/faab-bidding-system)). Canonical term: "FAAB," bids in "% of bank."
- **Waiver pickup (rolling waivers)** — claiming a free agent into a roster slot; on injury-driven pickups the operative rule is to act within hours of an Out/IR designation because the best replacement is claimed quickly ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/reading-injury-reports)). Canonical term: "waiver pickup" / "claim."
- **IR (Reserve/Injured List)** — multi-week sideline designation; minimum 4 games out, up to 8 return designations per team per season; a non-designated IR move before cutdowns ≈ season-ending ([PlaybookWire, 08/03/2026](https://playbookwire.com/nfl-injured-reserve-rules-how-ir-works-in-2026/)). Canonical term: "IR."
- **PPR (points per reception)** — scoring rule awarding a point for each reception; this league's scoring setting, which makes target/share and snap usage the primary value inputs for RBs/WRs (league configuration; see usage-based FAAB guidance in [Pro Football Network, undated](https://www.profootballnetwork.com/fantasy-football/burn-learn-10-fantasy-football-waiver-wire-faab-mistakes-youll-never-make-again/)). Canonical term: "PPR."
- **Flex** — the lineup slot that may be filled by any eligible position (here QB/RB/WR alongside the fixed slots); injury flags on a flex candidate are handled with the same base-rate-plus-contingency rule as other slots ([Waiver Wizard, 07/06/2026](https://fantasywaiverwizard.com/learn/start-sit-decisions)). Canonical term: "FLEX."
