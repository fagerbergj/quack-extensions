# Handcuffs and bye-week planning

Source: `research-r2-waivers.md` §1.4, §1.6.

## Handcuffs

A backup whose value is contingent: near-zero while the starter is healthy,
startable if the starter misses time — almost always at RB, since a backup
WR rarely inherits a full target share the way a backup RB inherits touches
([NovaPredict, Jun 10, 2026](https://novapredict.com/blog/fantasy-football-handcuffs)).

**Two screening questions:**

1. Is the contingent role concentrated? A true bell-cow backup takes
   70-75%+ of backfield touches if the starter is out.
2. Is the path clear — the unambiguous #2, not a committee member?

**Data-backed base rates** (2020-2024, [FantasyDutchman, 2025](https://thefantasydutchman.com/drafting-handcuff-rbs-valuable-or-just-a-myth/)):
≥33% of top-24 RB1s miss 3+ games to injury in a season; but only 23% of
drafted handcuffs (rounds 10-15) delivered 2+ startable weeks tied to the
starter's absence, and 59% were flat misses. Late-round WRs outscored
late-round RBs by ~18 fantasy points/season over the same window. No
published heuristic keys this data specifically to a 10-team league; the
report's only 10-team-specific anchors are the (n-1)/n ~90% bid-shading
math and that a 9-manager pool leaves fewer unowned backups to react with.

**10-team / short-bench caveat:** in a 10-team league with a short bench,
the waiver wire is deep enough to react to an injury when it happens —
handcuffs matter most in deep leagues where replacements are scarce
([NovaPredict, Jun 10, 2026](https://novapredict.com/blog/fantasy-football-handcuffs)).
Pre-injury stash at $0-2/minimum bid; after the injury it becomes the
25-40%-FAAB replacement-starter case (`faab-bidding.md`).

## Bye-week planning

- **Lead time:** add streaming coverage 1-2 weeks before the bye hits, not
  during the conflict week when the wire is thinnest ([Fantasy Strategy
  Guide, flagged secondary](https://fantasystrategyguide.com/bye-week-management)).
- **Double-stacked bye:** two rostered players (often same NFL team, or a
  stacked RB1+handcuff) sharing a bye creates a two-slot deficit in one week
  that is "rarely recoverable through waiver adds alone" in a 5-bench
  format — flag it as a draft/trade-time problem, not a same-week waiver fix.
- **Tier the response by player importance, not position:** a top-5-at-position
  bye is mandatory coverage; a solid-but-replaceable starter's bye is
  situational; a streamer/depth bye is the same decision already being made.
- **NFL byes cluster** — e.g. six teams sharing a Week 12 bye vs. two in
  Week 9 means far more waiver-wire competition in the heavier weeks
  ([Sleeper Blog, Aug 31, 2024](https://sleeper.com/blog/how-do-waivers-work-in-fantasy-football/)).
- **Counter-position:** the roster drafted in August is rarely the roster
  actually being managed when most byes arrive — don't over-invest in
  avoiding a single bad week at draft-time at the cost of better talent.
