# Streaming K and DEF

Source: `research-r2-waivers.md` §1.5. Applies regardless of waiver type
(FAAB or rolling) — the cost is always minimal, only the channel differs.

## Kickers

Consensus: **stream, spend near zero.** No position has a lower correlation
between draft slot and final rank or week-to-week scoring — "a dozen or
more starting kickers are sitting on waivers at any given time" ([Footballguys, Sep 15, 2026](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2026-rent-a-kicker-week02)).
Rank the *situation*, not the kicker: dome/indoor venue, high implied team
total, an offense that stalls in field-goal range.

- Cost: "a dollar or nothing, always" ([4for4, Aug 28, 2026](https://www.4for4.com/2026/preseason/ultimate-guide-winning-waiver-wire-2026)).
- Availability gate (sources conflict — surface both): ≥50% of leagues
  ([Footballguys](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2026-rent-a-kicker-week02))
  vs. ≤40% of Yahoo leagues ([4for4, Week 3 2025](https://www.4for4.com/2025/w3/fantasy-football-kicker-streaming-week-3-no-need-prater-fantasy-points)).
- Hold exceptions exist only at the edges: only two kickers (Aubrey,
  Dicker) finished top-10 in each of the prior two seasons, and they "seem
  to be the only ones worth a speculative pick" ([USA TODAY, Sep 2, 2025](https://www.usatoday.com/story/sports/fantasy/football/2025/09/02/fantasy-football-strategy-streaming-kickers/85927174007/)).
  A simulation study finds the honest answer is conditional: streaming wins
  in an uncoordinated league, holding a top/dome kicker wins if the whole
  league is chasing top scorers too ([Subvertadown](https://subvertadown.com/article/analysis-of-holding-vs-streaming-kickers)).
- One kicker is enough; replace at the bye rather than rostering a second.

## D/ST

Consensus, data-backed: **stream matchups, not talent.** Over the past three
seasons, top-5 scoring D/STs averaged 9.6 pts/game; D/STs facing the five
most favorable matchups averaged 10.4 — matchup beat talent in each of the
last four seasons ([ESPN 2026](https://www.espn.com.au/fantasy/football/story/_/id/49798496/2026-fantasy-football-d-st-road-map-early-season)).

- Cost: never more than $2, any week, any matchup, no exceptions ([4for4,
  Aug 28, 2026](https://www.4for4.com/2026/preseason/ultimate-guide-winning-waiver-wire-2026)).
- Ownership gate: <50% rostered = the streaming pool, >50% = "to avoid"
  ([ESPN 2026](https://www.espn.com.au/fantasy/football/story/_/id/49798496/2026-fantasy-football-d-st-road-map-early-season)).
- Refresh the read after weeks 4, 8, and 12 — NFL personnel/scheme changes
  swiftly enough that a preseason read goes stale.
- Hold exception: a schedule-elite D/ST that keeps scoring through weeks
  15-17 can be worth its premium in this league's playoff format (6 of 10
  teams, playoffs from week 15) — the experts frame this as a format
  decision, not a talent one.

## PPR note

PPR scoring changes nothing about K/DEF logic — the receptions bonus only
touches QB/RB/WR/TE scoring. Don't condition K/DEF advice on PPR.
