# Roster fit, win-now vs. depth, and playoff-position modifiers

Source: `report.md` (R3 v2) sections 1.3, 1.4, 2.3, 2.5.

## Fit can move the fair price 15-25%

[Draft Value Charts Explained (undated)](https://draftvalueanalytics.com/trade-value-charts-explained):
chart value "doesn't capture roster-need premium, which can legitimately
shift the fair trade price by 15 to 25 percent" on an imbalanced roster.

## The reversal rule

[FantasyStrategyGuide (undated)](https://fantasystrategyguide.com/trade-strategy-guide):
"a team already carrying 4 solid running backs gets no marginal benefit from
adding a 5th. Roster context can reverse the scarcity premium entirely." Its
execution rule: an offer "within 10-15% of fair value" that addresses a real
need should generally be executed rather than negotiated further.

## The mutual-need rule

[RSJ (Sep 15, 2026)](https://www.rotostreetjournal.com/2026-09-15/2026-fantasy-football-week-2-trade-value-chart/):
an offer that clearly benefits both teams' overall value — not just yours —
makes a partner cooperative; watch surplus positional depth across the
league's other rosters, not just your own weak spots. [FantasyPros Trading
101 (2026-07)](https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/07/fantasy-football-trading-101-how-to-make-a-trade-2026/):
duplicate-position trades are a named anti-pattern — trading for a
quarterback you already have a comparable starter at, or giving up your only
viable RB2, is not a good trade even if the numbers look even.

## Win-now (consolidate) vs. hold the star (depth)

Both positions are published, neither is universal — state which applies:

- **Consolidate for the stretch run:** [FFTradeAnalyzer, Playoff Trading
  Strategy (undated)](https://www.fftradeanalyzer.com/guides/playoff-trades):
  a contender in Weeks 10-14 is "optimizing for Weeks 15-17 only... trade
  depth for stars... prioritize ceiling over floor... reduce bench depth...
  handcuff your stars."
- **Hold the star, don't trade down:** [RSJ, "Anatomy of a Tier Down" (Aug 5,
  2026)](https://www.rotostreetjournal.com/2026-08-05/anatomy-tier-down/):
  "In shallow formats like a 10-team, start-9 league, weekly ceiling is
  everything. Stars win those titles. If you trade a blue-chip superstar for
  three average starting options, you just traded a dollar for four
  quarters" — this league's exact format (10-team, start-9) is the one the
  source names.

The report's reconciliation: consolidation fits teams at or inside the
playoff cutline; trade-down fits teams past their competitive window. Say
which record situation the recommendation assumes.

## The win-now premium is real, but usually doesn't decide the title

[Footballguys, Adam Harstad (Sep 21, 2024)](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2024-dynasty-in-theory-lets-talk-about-win-now-trades):
in a 12-team league, one guaranteed regular-season win lifts title odds by
about 2 percentage points; top score in Week 16/17 lifts title odds more
than 8pp, Week 15 more than 4pp — playoff-week points are worth roughly
4-8x a regular week for title purposes (12-team-tagged; this is a 6-of-10
format, which makes the playoffs harder to reach, but the exact multipliers
are not re-derived for 10 teams).

[South Harmon FF, T-Rock (Jan 22, 2025 cycle, 25 seasons, 56 analyzed
trades)](https://www.southharmonff.com/articles/playoff-odds-amp-strategy-1):
82.14% of analyzed playoff-window trades had zero impact on the outcome;
only 3.58% (2 of 56) directly affected a title. Read the two findings
together: Harstad measures win probability per unit of roster improvement
(a real but modest premium); South Harmon's near-zero-impact figure is
mostly about panic trades, not about what a good trade is worth.

## Rebuild pivot line

[CalculatorCollection playoff odds calculator (Sept 2026)](https://www.calculatorcollection.org/en/calculators/fantasy-sports/playoff-odds-calculator/):
"if playoff probability is below 15-20% with fewer than 5 weeks remaining,
the math generally suggests pivoting to a rebuild" rather than an all-in
push.

## Bad-advice patterns

Paying a scarcity premium for a 5th RB on a roster already carrying 4, or
buying a duplicate elite at an already-held position, is pattern #4. Pricing
a Week 13+ deal at full-season value while fighting for the bracket, or
demanding full-season value from a partner with under 15-20% playoff odds
and fewer than 5 weeks left, is pattern #5.
